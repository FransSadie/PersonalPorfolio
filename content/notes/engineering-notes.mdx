The same themes have come up across several of my recent projects:

- keep important user data local when the product can work without a server
- show how an automated decision was reached instead of hiding it behind a score
- validate AI output before it reaches the product or the database
- organise dense interfaces around the decision someone needs to make

Halo uses clear rules to explain scam risk. PSYCHED checks local-model output and applies safety rules before it saves coaching feedback. Market Lens turns price data into rankings, scans, and diagnostics for hands-on research. They are different products, but each one needs to show enough context for a person to understand what to do next.
