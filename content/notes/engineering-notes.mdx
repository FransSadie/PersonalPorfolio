My recent projects keep returning to the same engineering themes:

- keep important user data local when the product can work without a server
- make automated decisions inspectable instead of hiding them behind a score
- validate AI output before it reaches the product or the database
- design dense technical interfaces around the decision a person needs to make

Halo uses readable rules to explain scam risk. PSYCHED validates structured local-model output and adds safety gates before generating coaching. Market Lens turns raw price features into rankings, scans, and diagnostics for a human researcher. Different products, but the same principle: complex systems should expose enough context for people to trust their next action.
