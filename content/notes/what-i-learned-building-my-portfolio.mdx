The technology was the easy part. The harder question was what the site should feel like before someone reads a single paragraph.

I wanted it to feel technical and a little atmospheric without becoming busy or difficult to read. That led to:

- wider spacing
- a softer colour palette
- stronger typography
- fewer interface details, used more carefully

The main thing I learned is that a strong visual direction only helps when the site is still comfortable to read.
