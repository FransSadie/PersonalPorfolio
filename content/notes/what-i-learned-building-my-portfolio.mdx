The hardest part was not the stack. It was deciding what kind of **signal** the site should send before a visitor reads a single paragraph.

I wanted the interface to feel atmospheric and technical, but not cramped or performative. That pushed the design toward:

- wider spacing
- softer glows
- stronger typography
- fewer but more intentional interface details

The main lesson is that aesthetic direction only works when it improves the reading experience instead of competing with it.
